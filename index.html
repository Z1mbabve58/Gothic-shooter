HOW TO PLAY

🎯 COMBAT: Shoot skeletons in the HEAD (2x) or glowing HEART (3x). Knock off iron helmets with 1 headshot, and shatter heavy armor with the AXE (1 hit), RIFLE (5 shots), or SHOTGUN (1–2 shots).

🌑 DARKNESS: Leaving the campfire light deals damage every 5 seconds. Base damage grows with waves (1 HP on waves 1–3, 2 HP from wave 4, 3 HP from wave 7) and increases the deeper you go into the forest. Full Moon waves (5, 7, 10) light up the map safely!

🔥 CAMPFIRE & DEFENSE: Gather wood in the forest and press [E] near the fire to fuel it, or near barricades to repair them.

⌨️ CONTROLS: WASD (Move) | C/SHIFT (Slide) | SPACE (Jump) | RMB (Aim) | Q (Shoulder) | 1: Axe, 2: Rifle, 3: Shotgun | R (Reload) | ESC (Menu)
